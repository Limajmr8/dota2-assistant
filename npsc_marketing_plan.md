# NPSC Irresistible Offer Marketing Plan (30 Days)

## 1) Dream Outcome (Buyer Avatar + Promise)
- Target buyer: NPSC aspirants who want fast, structured prep and confidence for exam/interview.
- Core promise: **“Pass with a clear roadmap, not random studying.”**
- Positioning: This is for serious candidates who want a guided, exam-focused path.

## 2) Grand Slam Offer (Result-Focused Bundle)
Offer name: **NPSC Rank Accelerator System**

Includes:
- 200 high-probability NPSC questions (with answers/explanations)
- NPSC study strategy roadmap
- ChatGPT quiz prompts for active recall
- 7/14/30-day revision schedules
- Bonus: interview prep cheat-sheet

## 3) Value Equation Execution
- Dream Outcome: confidence, preparedness, and faster revision cycles.
- Perceived Likelihood: testimonials, sample pages, and progress screenshots.
- Time Delay: “Start in 10 minutes” onboarding + day-1 plan.
- Effort/Sacrifice: done-for-you structure so students don’t build plans from scratch.

## 4) Pricing + Offer Structure
- Intro price: **₹299** (first 20 buyers or deadline window).
- Price lift: **₹499** after proof milestone (20 buyers).
- Order bump / upsell:
  - Interview Rapid Revision Add-on
  - Last 7 Days Crash Revision Plan

## 5) Lead Generation Engine
- Daily lead target: **30–50** relevant prospects.
- Primary channels:
  - Facebook exam groups
  - Telegram/WhatsApp exam communities
  - Short-form content with CTA to sample + buy link
- Lead magnet: free mini sample (10 questions + 2-day prep plan).

## 6) Conversion System (DM + Landing Copy)
- Message formula:
  - Pain: scattered prep, low confidence
  - Promise: structured, NPSC-focused prep
  - Proof: screenshots/testimonials/sample
  - CTA: instant payment + instant access
- Follow-up cadence:
  - Day 0: first response + link
  - Day 1: reminder + benefit restatement
  - Day 3: proof/testimonial follow-up
  - Day 5: final follow-up + urgency

## 7) Ethical Risk Reversal
- Guarantee language:
  - “If this is not useful in your first 3 days, message for support/refund policy as stated.”
- Principle: clarity + certainty + proof, not manipulation.

## 8) Weekly KPI Dashboard Rules
Track weekly:
- Leads contacted
- Conversations started
- Conversion rate
- Sales count
- Revenue
- Best-performing message and channel

Action rule:
- Scale only channels/messages with the highest conversion rate.

## 9) 30-Day Execution Cadence
- Week 1: finalize offer, lead magnet, scripts, first testimonials.
- Week 2: increase posting + DM volume, test 3 hooks.
- Week 3: raise price per rule, add upsell, use proof-heavy creatives.
- Week 4: optimize funnel, retarget warm leads, standardize operations.

## 10) Positioning Standard
- Make buyers feel smart for choosing a structured path.
- Avoid shame language (“you’re dumb if you say no”).
- Use this angle:
  - “If you want a faster, guided route to NPSC prep, this is the most complete starter system at this price.”
