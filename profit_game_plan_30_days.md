# 30-Day Profit Execution Game Plan

Run both income streams in parallel:
- Stream A: NPSC one-time sales
- Stream B: Church content monthly subscriptions

## Core Rule
Every day must include:
1. One sales action for NPSC
2. One acquisition/retention action for church content
3. End-of-day metrics update

## Week 1 (Setup + First Sales)
- Finalize and keep live:
  - `NPSC_Crack_Kit.pdf`
  - Gumroad listing
  - Facebook first-3-group posting queue
- Launch church starter offer:
  - “Monthly Sermon + Social Pack”
  - Intro monthly price (set and keep consistent for first cohort)
- DM outreach target:
  - 30 NPSC prospects/day
  - 10 church leads/day
- KPI focus:
  - First 5 NPSC buyers
  - First 3 church subscribers

## Week 2 (Distribution Scale)
- NPSC:
  - Post in 5 additional relevant groups
  - Repost top-performing angle from Week 1
- Church:
  - Publish sample pack preview
  - Offer 7-day trial or first-month discount
- KPI focus:
  - Reach 20 NPSC buyers (price switch trigger)
  - Reach 8 church subscribers

## Week 3 (Conversion Optimization)
- NPSC:
  - Switch to ₹499 after buyer #20
  - Add urgency in copy (“intro window closed”)
- Church:
  - Start weekly testimonial cycle
  - Add referral ask from existing subscribers
- KPI focus:
  - Maintain conversion rate at new price
  - Reach 12 church subscribers

## Week 4 (Retention + Repeatability)
- NPSC:
  - Bundle upsell: “Interview/Revision Add-on”
- Church:
  - Deliver month-end premium pack early
  - Collect retention feedback from subscribers
- KPI focus:
  - Predictable weekly NPSC sales baseline
  - 80%+ church subscriber retention

## Daily Non-Negotiables
- [ ] Publish 1 NPSC post or DM batch
- [ ] Publish/send 1 church offer or follow-up batch
- [ ] Log all numbers in tracker
- [ ] Decide one improvement for next day
