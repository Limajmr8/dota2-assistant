# Church Content Business Playbook

## Offer
Product: **Monthly Church Content Pack**

Pack includes:
- 4 sermon outlines
- 12 social media captions
- 8 short prayer/devotional scripts
- 4 announcement templates

## Target Customers
- Local churches
- Youth ministries
- Small fellowship leaders

## Subscription Model
- Monthly recurring plan
- Clear delivery date each month (same day each month)
- Renewal reminder 3 days before next cycle

## Launch Sequence
1. Build a one-page sample preview (free)
2. Share preview with first 20 leads
3. DM script:
   - Identify need
   - Send sample
   - Offer monthly subscription
4. Onboard first cohort with welcome message + delivery calendar

## Weekly Operating Cadence
- Monday: Lead generation (new churches + ministries)
- Tuesday: Outreach + follow-up
- Wednesday: Close calls / payment collection
- Thursday: Content production
- Friday: Delivery + feedback
- Saturday: Testimonials + referrals

## Retention System
- Send deliverables on time every month
- Include one “bonus” file for active subscribers
- Ask for one testimonial per month
- Track churn reason for every cancelation

## Minimum KPIs
- New leads/week
- Calls/DM conversations/week
- New subscribers/week
- Monthly churn count
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
